02

The weekly rhythm

MONDAY

Red Flag & attendance

  • Prepare the Red Flag tracker before 12 noon for members with zero attendance in the last seven days.
  • Complete Head Coach review, refresh after review, and confirm the messaging owner and approach.
  • Complete the weekly attendance process and verify the Attendance Dashboard.
  • Review repeat flags and record all approved reach-outs.
WEDNESDAY

Orange Flag & reconnecting

  • Prepare the tracker for members with zero sessions so far this week.
  • Head Coach review: 10:15–10:45am. Refresh after sign-off before messaging.
  • Kickstarter members receive Head Coach calls only.
  • If there is no Head Coach response by noon, hold the SMS and ask the Operations Manager via Slack for approval.
FRIDAY

Close the reporting week

  • Review the completed Friday–Thursday period.
  • Bring attendance, membership, cancellation and lead reports together.
  • Check source data and reconcile unexpected totals.
  • Send Melissa Walsh a concise report with changes, actions and items needing review.
Date check: lead reports use Friday–Thursday in Brisbane time. Historical Membership uses Friday–Thursday weeks and calendar months. A current incomplete period is period-to-date. Always check the workbook’s displayed period and “Counted As At” or export date.

04

Before a message goes out

Red Flag · Monday

  • Harry Ritter confirms the Messaging Owner and message approach before sending.
  • Repeat Red Flag members need the Head Coach’s confirmed approach.
  • Kickstarter and Sponsored members are excluded. Thailand Tour is excluded when it is the sole membership.
  • Use one approved adult message per member or parent message per family; replace all placeholders and send via Clubworx SMS.

Orange Flag · Wednesday

  • Send only after Head Coach approval and Refresh After Review.
  • Kickstarter members: Head Coach calls only. Sponsored members: no SMS or call.
  • For repeat Orange Flag members, ask the Head Coach whether a personal call is needed.
  • Choose one of the five approved messages and replace [Name] where present. Log the reach-out and reply.
Escalate with context: flag unclear data, failed refreshes, repeated non-attendance and cancellation risk. Include the member or report involved, what was checked and the decision needed. Send urgent member or payment issues to Melissa Walsh. Respect every Head Coach removal.

06

Leave the next step visible

A useful handover includes

  1. What changed: the member issue, report movement or task result.
  2. What was checked: source export, reporting dates and relevant notes.
  3. Who owns it: the confirmed person responsible for follow-up.
  4. What happens next: the next action and any approval still needed.

Before closing the task

  • The relevant tracker is updated.
  • The message or call outcome is recorded.
  • Unresolved items are visible to the next person.
  • Friday reporting is brief, specific and based on checked figures.
